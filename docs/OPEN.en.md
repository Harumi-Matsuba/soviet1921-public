How digits are written (whether the 1x or 5x pairs are digits) cannot be settled from two messages.
- The heading of Nr 523 is "FUNKSPRUCH Nr 523 VOM 14 JUNI 1921" (frame 0432). As the message is dated 14 June, [16] cannot be the plain number "16th". Pair 16 is therefore not the number written as it is. Reading "1x = digit x" gives "letter of 6 June", which fits the date, but the only other pair of this form is [18], so this cannot be checked (added 11 November 2026).

## Wrong letters (one letter each)
- Nr 451: то[ф]гого (торгового; letters are missing as well), все[м] (всех), бога[]ыи (т is missing; the pair before the gap, 81 = ы, is the one counted), ревизионо[з] and ревизионо[н] (ending of ревизионной), высл[50]ть (а; the digit is overstruck on the sheet and could be 5, 7, 0 or 9), до[54]лада (к = 64).
- Nr 523: опре[о]эленоэ (д = 94), п[д]ложениэ (о = 95), окон[ю]ания (ч = 84), ко[у]у (м = 05). All four have the shape of a 4/5 confusion in the second digit. Two readers read the typed sheet alike, so the error is taken to lie in the sending or the interception (an inference).

## Transcription
- Nr 523 has 575 digits, one short of 24 x 24 (last row of the last column).
- On frame 0432 "60" is typed between the 9th and 10th lines, above the group 04304. The text reads through without it, and where it belongs is not known.
- On frame 0427, 12th line, 2nd group, an "I" is typed above the first digit. It is read as 1, giving 16709 (which yields 10 = full stop, and the sentence reads).

## Printed editions (is the plaintext of these messages printed?)
As of 11 November 2026 it has not been checked whether the plaintext of these messages is printed.
- *Dokumenty vneshnei politiki SSSR*, vol. 4 (March-December 1921; 1960): held on paper by the National Diet Library, Japan (NDL Search R100000002-I000000394145-i13074848). No digitised copy was found.
- *Bol'shevistskoe rukovodstvo. Perepiska 1912-1927* (1996): a catalogue record was found; the book was not seen.
- *Sovetsko-germanskie otnosheniia* (1971): no holding found.
- Corroboration of the content (not the plaintext): Lenin's letter to Lutovinov of 30 May 1921 (Collected Works vol. 45) has "Kopp was at the head of the old mission. Kopp was removed, Stomonyakov has remained."; a note of June 1921 has "It looks as if Kopp has to be removed." and "This decision is being sabotaged by Lutovinov and now by Stomonyakov as well." (marxists.org, works/1921/may/30b.htm and jun/04b.htm). This fits the persons, the time and the dispute of Nr 451 and 523.
